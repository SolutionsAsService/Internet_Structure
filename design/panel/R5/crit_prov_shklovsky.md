Critic: Provocateur (Shklovsky), R5

- Anchor used: Shklovsky, "Art as Device": defamiliarization; roughen perception; automatization devours.
- Observed: r5__1280.png: dark slab, vivid fibre hues, red chain cutting the Carry band, stacked-bar underline on the headline; the figure's coloured washes sit almost invisibly on near-black. r5_strata_1280.png: seven pastel-washed cards, each with a top rule, in one grid. r5_history_1280.png: a uniformly beige page, one accent for every year numeral.

Findings:
1. [claim] The night slab estranges. Entering a cable jacket, with the seven hues vivid against black, makes the key's colours felt rather than read. The red chain and underline bars remain the device.
2. [defect] The 8% washes in the figure are close to invisible on #0e1117; the "coloured cross-section" is carried by the dots, not the washes. Minor; the sheet is not a new automatism there.
3. [claim] Strata cards (r5_strata_1280.png) are a new automatism. Seven same-size tinted boxes, each with a top rule and a bar, is the familiar dashboard card grid recoloured. The colour labels the category and adds no strangeness.
4. [claim] History in copper reads as a theme swap, not a device. Every year numeral is the same rust (r5_history_1280.png); it is a mood, and a sepia mood is a cliche of "the past".
5. [premise] Owner asked for variety; variety by zone-theming is the habitual solution. Not a defect against the owner's brief.

Verdict: PASS (the hero estranges; the rest is competent, familiar, and not a defect)

Requested change: None required. Optional: let the strata cards break rhythm (one card larger by organisation count) so that size, not tint, carries the difference.
