Critic: Provocateur (Debord)

- Anchor used: Debord, Society of the Spectacle, thesis 1-4 and 34: the spectacle is a social relation mediated by images; the image becomes capital. Demand a claim, not a display.

- Observed: Site is one route: a 274-node force graph beside a "Network brief" panel (evidence.md). Header badges read "NETWORK READY" and "LIVE GRAPH / REPOSITORY DATA"; panel stats are NODES 274, LINKS 648, LAYERS 52, ISOLATED 38; the side panel says "Select a node" and offers nothing before a click. 1280 labels ~6-7 px, 375 labels ~3-4 px (r0__375.png: dots with no legible words). 12 dossiers exist and none is shown. Legend: one blue "Infrastructure" dot against ~20 colours. Footer: "231 UNRESOLVED REFERENCES" and "NO EXTERNAL DATA REQUESTS".

- Findings:
F1 [premise] The site is a dashboard of the internet: "Global Infrastructure Atlas", "Node Intelligence", "Network Ready" perform monitoring of a system nobody here monitors; the status light is pure decor.
F2 [claim] No thesis anywhere on first screen: the page never says what the structure of the internet means, who owns it, or who is excluded. The map shows that things connect, not what the connection costs.
F3 [defect] Hairball of 274 dots and 648 links is contemplation, not relation; at 375 the labels (~3-4 px) are unreadable, so the spectacle is only coloured beads.
F4 [defect] Colour encodes ~20 categories with a single legend entry; the viewer is asked to admire, not decode.
F5 [premise] The real content, the 12 dossiers (AT&T, Bell System, RBOC, ARPANET history of ownership and breakup), is hidden while the picture of the picture is shipped (652 KB JS, empty HTML with a spinner).
F6 [claim] "231 unresolved references" and "38 isolated" are shown as proud statistics; the site confesses its own gaps as ornament instead of acting on them.
F7 [defect] Stat tiles (274/648/52/38) are numerals without a judgment; the dashboard-as-decoration.

- Verdict: OBJECT

- Requested change: Replace the landing with one stated claim about who built and owns the network (drawn from the Bell/AT&T/RBOC dossiers), and make the graph serve it: few named nodes, legible labels, one argued path. Delete status badges and decorative stat tiles; surface the dossiers as the text; fix or drop unresolved references rather than advertise them.
