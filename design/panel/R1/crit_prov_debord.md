- Anchor used: Debord, Society of the Spectacle: the spectacle is appearance standing in for relation; a work must claim and prove, not display.

- Observed: Landing at 1280 (r1__1280.png): h1 "No one owns the internet. 253 organisations hold it up." Kicker above it: "An atlas of the internet's owners". Figure: 253 grey dots, same visible size, in 7 bands; one red polyline Comcast > Equinix > Grace Hopper > Google > Verisign > ICANN, dashed Google > NVIDIA > TSMC. /about says the route is "chosen so that it touches all seven strata" and "shows dependency, not a measured traceroute". Evidence: 181 references unresolved, "left out of the map"; atlas x-position is meaningless.

Findings:
F1. [claim] The h1 asserts "no one owns" while the kicker says "owners" and the figure names Comcast, Google, ICANN. The data has no ownership or control field. The thesis is never argued; the pairing is a slogan.
F2. [claim] "253 organisations hold it up" is a count of dots, not a dependency finding. Nothing shown says what falls if one is removed. The number is an artifact of merging 274 records (evidence: 21 duplicates).
F3. [defect] Caption says "Dot size is the importance score (1-10)", but the screenshot's dots look uniform, so the visible figure does not show what the caption says.
F4. [premise] The red line is authored for looks: a route picked to touch every band, which is a mere illustration of the thesis. Its "every step is a recorded link" proves only that a story can be told, not that the structure is that story.
F5. [claim] The hero's "recorded connections" hides that 181 references were dropped; the gaps sit on /about#gaps, away from the figure that claims completeness.
F6. [premise] The dots, the History band and the 253-organisation scale are display weight. The 11 history ticks carry no relation to the strata above; the figure juxtaposes and never connects.
F7. [defect] Ideology of "work as map": the interactive atlas offers navigation as the only relation, and its layout is declared meaningless on its own page.

- Verdict: OBJECT

- Requested change: Make one claim and make the figure prove it. Either drop "No one owns" and show what concentrates (who sits on the single points of failure, ownership or control links where the data has them), or retitle to what is measured. Size the dots by importance visibly, or fix the caption. Put the 181 dropped references and the "chosen route" disclosure beside the figure, not on /about.
