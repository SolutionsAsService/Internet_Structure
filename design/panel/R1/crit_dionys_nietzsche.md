# Critic: Dionysian Excess (Nietzsche), R1

- Anchor used: The Birth of Tragedy: Apollo (form, measure, clarity) must be wedded to Dionysus (intoxication, excess, the rush of the whole); form without force is mere ledger.

- Observed: Landing 1280: huge condensed headline with "253 ORGANISATIONS" in red; a figure of seven equal-height ruled bands (about 70 px each), 253 small grey dots, one thin red polyline of six labelled steps plus a dashed branch. Everything sits on a calm paper ground inside a ruled header, rule and caption ("Tap a stratum to open it. Dot size is the dataset's importance score (1-10)."). Evidence: smallest SVG text 14 px (1280) and 12 px (375); 0 overflow, 0 contrast failures. At 375 the "Bell System 1877" label visibly collides with "ARPANET 1969" on the history axis.

- Findings:
F1 [claim] The Apollonian is complete (0 overflow, 0 contrast, 278 links, 0 broken); the Dionysian is one red line. Craft is evidenced, force is not.
F2 [defect] The 253 dots, the whole point of "the rush of the whole", are the palest, smallest marks on the screen: grey on paper, stacked in tidy rows, so multitude reads as a table of counts, not a swarm.
F3 [premise] Bands are all the same height though they hold 11 to 77 organisations. Equal measure flattens the Carry band's mass (77) against Serve (11); nothing swells, nothing overflows.
F4 [defect] The red route carries the only intoxication yet is a single path of six named firms; the other 247 orgs are mute grey beside it.
F5 [defect] At 375 the history labels overlap (Bell System 1877 over ARPANET 1969): measure failing, not excess.
F6 [claim] Atlas x-axis is declared meaningless ("read the lines, not the distances"); the evidence says the wild layout was reduced to a ledger by disclaimer.

- Verdict: OBJECT

- Requested change: Let the multitude overflow its measure. Scale band height or dot density to count so Carry visibly dwarfs Serve; let dots grow dark and crowd to the band edge with the red route cutting through them; stop at one saturated, unquiet field. Fix the 375 history label collision.
